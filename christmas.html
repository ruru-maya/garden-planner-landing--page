Before you give

Does the gift renew automatically?

No. A gift is a one-time year. The person you give it to decides whether to continue afterwards.

What if they don't know the app?

You can try the free demo yourself first, no signup needed, and see what you are giving.

What's the difference between Basic and Premium?

Premium includes everything in Basic plus the AI features: Magic Gardener layouts built around their patch, household and gardening level, the Plant Expert for pests, diseases and organic tips, and AI planting timelines.

How long do I have to activate my codes?

Activate the paid gift within 12 months of purchase, and your free buyer bonus within 6 months. Your full year or bonus month starts on activation. Longer mandatory validity periods remain unaffected. Read the gift terms.

What if CozyGrow permanently closes?

If the service permanently closes before a valid paid gift is redeemed, we will refund the purchaser. If it closes during a prepaid gift year, we will refund the unused prepaid portion. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Can I give it to myself?

Of course. Gift codes work for any account, including your own.
