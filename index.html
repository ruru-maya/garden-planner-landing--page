Before you wander in

A few things
you might wonder.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo opens the real app with starter plants and a planted demo patch. Explore as a guest first; create an account to keep your garden beyond 72 hours.

Is CozyGrow Garden free?

You can explore the demo without paying. The app also offers a free account and paid plans with different limits and features. You can review the available plans inside the app before choosing.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. CozyGrow Garden runs in your browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Start by opening the demo garden.

Is it just for autumn?

CozyGrow is a companion for every growing season. Autumn is a lovely time to gather your notes, organize your seeds, and start thinking about the next thing you’d like to grow.
